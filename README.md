Penta is a social platform designed for music enthusiasts to discover, discuss, and share the music they love. It provides a dedicated space for fans, critics, and artists to connect through their shared passion for music.

## What is Penta?

In a digital world saturated with content, Penta aims to create a focused community for music lovers. Much like dedicated platforms for film or books, Penta is a central hub for everything related to music. It's a place to log your listening history, review new and old favorites, and discover hidden gems through a community of like-minded individuals. Our goal is to deepen the connection between listeners and the art they cherish.

## Core Features

*   **Review and Rate:** Share your thoughts on albums, songs, and artists. Rate them, write detailed reviews, and keep a log of your musical journey.
*   **Build Your Library:** Organize your favorite music into a personal library. Create custom lists and collections to share with others.
*   **Community Hubs:** Join or create communities based on genres, artists, or any music-related topic. Share opinions, post comments, and engage in discussions.
*   **Artist Profiles:** We envision a space where bands and artists can have verified profiles, offering a direct channel to connect with their audience.
*   **Seamless Listening:** With planned integration for services like Spotify, Deezer, and Tidal, you'll be able to listen to full albums and songs without ever leaving the site.

## Future Vision

Penta aims to grow into a comprehensive cultural hub. We plan to introduce curated spaces for other art forms such as cinema, visual arts, and literature, each accompanied by a specially selected musical ambiance to enhance the experience.

## Technology Stack

Penta is being built with a modern and robust technology stack:

*   **Frontend:** [React](https://react.dev/) and [Vite](https://vitejs.dev/)
*   **Backend:** A Node.js backend is currently under development to support our growing list of features.
